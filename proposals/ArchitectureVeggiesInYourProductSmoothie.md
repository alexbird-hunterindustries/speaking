# Architecture Veggies in your Product Smoothie
## How to make progress on the expensive architecture changes without stressing out your business stakeholders

They want a thing. You know you need three Rather Complicated backend components to build the thing. If you start today,
there will be a lot of days or weeks of hard, thankless, invisible backend work to do before you have anything to show
for it. Your stakeholders would be stressed the whole time, and that stress would transfer to you as requests for frequent
updates and reminders about the urgency.

When we hide-and-architect in the backend, we're trying to eat a kale-beetroot-water smoothie, and it doesn't go down so 
well. Instead, we could be slicing up your architectural veggies and mixing them with tasty fruit, so the smoothie goes down better.

In this talk, I'll work through an example feature list, showing how you can deliver something today while making steady
progress on the infrastructure, backend, or architectural changes that are required to build the features responsibly.
