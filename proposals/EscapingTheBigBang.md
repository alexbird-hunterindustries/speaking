# Escaping the Big Bang
## Patterns for uncovering the next most important slice in an "all or nothing" world

Many of us work with stakeholders or leaders who want a big-bang release -- "once you've finished these ten things, then
let's talk". However, we have learned as an industry that frequent feedback and small inventory make a big, positive difference
to our success. How can you figure out what is the next most valuable little slice to build when none of your stakeholders 
and domain experts will tell you?

I will present a series of patterns for cracking through the "all or nothing" mentality to find a small slice of
functionality that you can build this week. Patterns include "Follow the Gripes", and "Count the manual person-hours".
